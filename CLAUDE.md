# CLAUDE.md

## Writing field notes in Jerrell's voice

When writing or editing a post in `src/content/notes/`, write it the way
Jerrell talks, not the way a magazine writes. His authentic voice is in
`building-a-coffee-bar.md` and in the raw notes he pastes into chat — use
those as the reference, and reuse his own sentences wherever possible.

### Voice

- First person, conversational, like telling a friend what happened. Plain,
  direct sentences.
- Tell it in story order: the situation, why it bugged us, what I built, how
  it went.
- "We/our house" for household decisions, "I" for the building and the tools.
- Casual connectors are his style: "So", "Naturally", "Recently",
  "Next thing you know", "And just like that".
- Honest and self-deprecating about mistakes, never dramatic about them:
  "Lots of mistakes were made, lots of learnings." "I either purchased cheap
  forstner bits or I really needed a drill press."
- Small asides and exclamations are welcome: "Cry tears of joy", "Kind of a
  two-for-one hack, if you will", "Oh!", "Enjoy the pictures below!"
- Everyday vocabulary stays: "laying around", "a bunch", "etc." Don't polish
  it away.

### Avoid

- Magazine turns of phrase ("the gap finally earns its keep", "you can see
  where this is going").
- Fancy verbs and imagery ("swallows laptops", "riding high enough to grab").
- Stacked em-dash clauses, alliteration, clever kickers at the end of
  sections.
- Headings that try to be cute. Keep them plain: "The install", "The parts",
  "What's next?".

### Facts

- Jerrell's own words are the source of truth. If he pastes an AI-written
  draft alongside his own thoughts, his thoughts win every conflict.
- Never carry a number, dimension, product name, or detail into a post unless
  he stated it or it's clearly visible in his photos. If a detail is only in
  an AI draft, leave it out and ask.

### Structure

- Short posts: a handful of short paragraphs, 2-4 plain headings max.
- Photo captions are short and literal, like labels: "Toe kick", "Drawer
  box", "Dry fitting the bin". No mini-essays in captions.
- Alt text is the exception — keep it fully descriptive for accessibility.
- End simply and warmly, often pointing at the gallery or listing what's
  next.
